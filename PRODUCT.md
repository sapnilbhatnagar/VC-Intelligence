# PRODUCT.md

register: mixed (brand for marketing/landing surfaces, product for the app shell)

## Product Purpose

VC Intelligence runs an 8-stage AI pipeline that turns a single company name into
an institutional-grade due-diligence package: company research, market sizing,
a financial model, a risk score, comparable deals, an investor memo, a formatted
report, and a visual one-pager. The work that takes an analyst a week happens in
minutes.

## Users

Small teams with large mandates: micro VC funds screening their whole inbound
pile, private equity teams doing pre-LOI triage with a paper trail, and
strategic buyers tracking acquisition targets. They are numerate, skeptical,
and allergic to fluff. They trust tools that look like the documents they
already respect (an IC memo, a research note), not consumer SaaS.

## Tone

Rigorous, quiet, confident. Reads like a research desk, not a growth-hacked
startup. Specific over grand: name the eight stages, show the actual output,
never say "revolutionary" or "powered by cutting-edge AI." No emoji, no tick or
cross glyphs anywhere in product copy; data-quality markers are the bracketed
words [CONFIRMED] / [ESTIMATED] / [UNKNOWN].

## Anti-references (what to avoid)

- The fintech reflex: navy-and-gold, glass cards, big-number hero metrics.
- The AI-tool reflex: electric-blue-on-black, neon glow, sparkle-AI everywhere.
- Identical icon+heading+text card grids repeated down the page.
- Gradient text, decorative glassmorphism, side-stripe accent borders.

## Strategic principles

- Show the deliverable. The strongest selling point is the memo itself, so put a
  specimen of the output on screen rather than describing it.
- Typography and layout carry the design; color stays restrained.
- The one accent is a deep cobalt (#1D4ED8), used for primary actions, active
  state, and focus only. The BUY / HOLD / PASS verdicts keep their own semantic
  data scale (green / amber / red), independent of the brand accent.
- One light theme: "institutional daylight." Cool-gray canvas, white tiles with
  hairline borders and soft shadows (Apple-style structure), disciplined
  semantic color (Google-style data vocabulary). See DESIGN.md.
- The pipeline is the product's signature: render it as a connected agent rail
  (the agent visibly moves stage to stage), and name the engine working each
  stage.
